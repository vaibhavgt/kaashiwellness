Before you book

Important notice

  • We offer only genuine Ayurvedic and internationally recognized therapeutic services.
  • All treatments are availed at the guest's own discretion.
  • Please inform our staff of any medical condition before the treatment.
  • Pregnant guests and those with health concerns should consult a doctor prior to booking.
  • Treatments are for general wellness and relaxation and are not a substitute for medical care.

Appointments & etiquette

  • Please arrive 15 minutes before your scheduled session.
  • Cancellations need at least 4 working hours' notice; late cancellations attract a 50% fee.
  • Mobile phones must be kept on silent mode.
  • Guests below 16 years are not permitted inside the spa.
  • Smoking and alcohol consumption are strictly prohibited.

Professional conduct

  • Disposable undergarments are provided; therapist draping ensures privacy throughout.
  • All therapies are strictly professional.
  • Any inappropriate behavior will result in immediate termination of the session, full charges, and possible legal action.
